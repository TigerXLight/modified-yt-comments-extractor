# MSN Source Adapter Final Goal Lock

The MSN adapter goal is not merely to add tests. The goal is to preserve a usable MSN source adapter chain covering:

- article extraction,
- comments and profile extraction,
- offline viewer/archive outputs,
- media image/video registration and download status,
- source-role and primary-source-status provenance,
- final live-evidence promotion only after a positive live result.

This lock should be preserved during future roadmap work.
