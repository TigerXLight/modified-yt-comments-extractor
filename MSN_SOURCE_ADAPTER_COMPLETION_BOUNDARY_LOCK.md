# MSN Source Adapter Completion Boundary Lock

This lock preserves the correct final language.

## Do not say

- `MSN is complete` from no-network self-tests alone.
- `Live MSN validation passed` without a filled positive live evidence result.
- `MSN is primary source` merely because a page was captured on MSN.

## Say

- `Repository and operator tooling are ready for live evidence`.
- `Release candidate locked pending live evidence`.
- `Complete with positive live evidence` only after the live evidence validator passes.

## Source-chain boundary

MSN may be the captured surface or republisher. The visible publisher, visible media credit, wire/service credit, original source, and primary source status must remain separate.
